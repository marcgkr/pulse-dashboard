Is a thread lift right for you?

A lift now, firmer skin later

A thread lift works in two stages. That's why results keep improving for a while after you leave the clinic.

  1. Assessment

    Dr Ram looks at where your face has descended, how much skin laxity there is, and whether threads are the right tool for it.

  2. The lift

    Under local anaesthetic, threads are placed beneath the skin and used to gently reposition sagging tissue. You see the lift straight away.

  3. The collagen stage

    Over the following months the threads dissolve and stimulate collagen, which improves firmness and skin quality.

Who it tends to suit

The biggest misconception is that a lift has to mean surgery and weeks hiding at home. For the right face, threads do the job with far less downtime. For others, Dr Ram will say so.

Often a good fit

  • Early to moderate jowls and a softening jawline
  • Cheeks that have dropped and deepened the smile lines
  • People who want a lift but can't take weeks off work
  • Anyone who wants a natural result, not a pulled look

Probably not the right tool

  • Very loose, heavy skin that needs surgical removal
  • Volume loss with little sagging, where filler may do more
  • Anyone wanting a permanent change

Questions people ask

How much downtime should I plan for?

Most people are back to their daily routine within a few days. Swelling, tenderness or bruising can happen early on. Dr Ram will walk you through what recovery looks like for the areas you treat.

Does it hurt?

The treatment areas are numbed with local anaesthetic first. You may feel pressure and some tenderness afterwards.

Will it look natural?

The aim is to put tissue back where it used to sit, so you look refreshed rather than changed. Dr Ram plans the direction of each thread around your face.

How long do results last?

It depends on the threads used, the areas treated and your skin. Dr Ram will give you a realistic timeframe at consultation.

Can I combine it with fillers or skin boosters?

Sometimes threads and filler work well together, one for lift and one for volume. That's decided after Dr Ram examines you.
