Assets for the Line Aesthetics landing pages.

Images are the clinic's own, taken from lineaestheticsclinic.com, threadlift.lineaestheticsclinic.com
and nosethreadlift.lineaestheticsclinic.com, resized to WebP. Only Dr Ram Nath and model/patient
imagery is used; no other practitioner appears.

Hero videos (hero-nose.mp4, hero-face.mp4, hero-ha.mp4) are 5-second loops generated in Higgsfield
(Kling 3.0) from the clinic's own model photos.
