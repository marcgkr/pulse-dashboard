Why hyaluronic acid

What it is

  • A gel based on a substance your skin already contains
  • Gradually broken down by the body over time
  • Can be dissolved with an enzyme if ever needed

Questions people ask

Does it hurt?

Many HA fillers contain a local anaesthetic, and numbing can be used beforehand. Most people feel brief pinches and pressure.

How long does filler last?

It depends on the product and the area. Areas that move a lot, like the lips, tend to fade sooner. Dr Ram will tell you what to expect for yours.

Will I look overdone?

Not if the plan is right. Dr Ram works in proportion with your features and would rather recommend less product than too much.

Is there downtime?

Most people return to their day straight after. Mild swelling or bruising can show for a few days, especially under the eyes and in the lips.

I've never had filler. Where should I start?

Tell us the area that bothers you most in the questions above. Dr Ram will advise whether filler is the right choice for it at all.
