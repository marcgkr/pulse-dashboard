Suitability

Filler is not one size fits all

The same syringe placed in a different spot gives a very different result. That’s why the plan starts with your face, not the product.

It can

  • Soften under-eye hollows and shadows
  • Restore cheek volume and support
  • Shape and balance the lips
  • Define the chin and jawline

It won’t

  • Lift sagging skin, where threads may do more
  • Replace good skincare and sleep
  • Look right without a proper assessment

Questions

What people ask Dr Ram

Does it hurt?

Many HA fillers contain a local anaesthetic, and numbing can be used beforehand. Most people feel brief pinches and pressure.

How long does filler last?

It depends on the product and the area. Areas that move a lot, like the lips, tend to fade sooner. Dr Ram will tell you what to expect for yours.

Will I look overdone?

Not if the plan is right. Dr Ram works in proportion with your features and would rather recommend less product than too much.

Is there downtime?

Most people return to their day straight after. Mild swelling or bruising can show for a few days, especially under the eyes and in the lips.

Can filler be reversed?

Hyaluronic acid filler can be dissolved with an enzyme if ever needed, which is one reason Dr Ram uses it.
