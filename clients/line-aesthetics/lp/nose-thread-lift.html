Is a nose thread lift right for you?

What a nose thread lift can do

Threads give structure where the nose needs it. They work with your own anatomy, so the result looks like your nose, refined.

It can

  • Raise a low or flat bridge
  • Lift and sharpen a rounded or drooping tip
  • Straighten the profile line from forehead to tip
  • Give a defined look with no visible scars

It won't

  • Reduce a large hump or narrow bone the way surgery can
  • Last forever. Results typically hold for 12 to 18 months
  • Suit every nose. Dr Ram will tell you honestly if yours isn't a fit

Three checks before any thread goes in

The Asian nose often has thicker skin, softer tip cartilage and a lower bridge. That changes how many threads are needed and where they go, so Dr Ram assesses each of these first.

Skin thickness

Thicker skin over the bridge can need more threads for a visible lift.

Tip cartilage

How firm the tip is, and any deviation, decides what threads can realistically achieve.

Blood vessels

The most important safety check, especially if you've had surgery or filler before.

How the session works

  1. Assessment

    Dr Ram examines your nose and talks through what threads can and can't change for you.

  2. Threads placed

    Under local anaesthetic, fine dissolvable threads are guided along the bridge and into the tip. About 20 to 30 minutes.

  3. Settling

    You see the lift straight away. Over the following weeks collagen forms around the threads as they dissolve.

Questions people ask

Does it hurt?

The nose is numbed with local anaesthetic before threads are placed. Most people describe pressure rather than pain. Dr Ram will explain what to expect for you at the consult.

What is the downtime?

Most people return to normal activities within a day. Some swelling or bruising is possible in the first few days.

How long do results last?

Typically 12 to 18 months. A top-up before the result fully fades usually needs fewer threads than a first session.

I've had nose filler or surgery before. Can I still do this?

Possibly. Previous procedures change the blood vessel and tissue picture, which is why Dr Ram checks this carefully first. Tell us in the questions above.

Thread lift or filler for the nose?

Threads give lift and definition, especially at the tip. Filler adds volume. Some noses do best with one, some with both. Dr Ram will recommend after examining you.
