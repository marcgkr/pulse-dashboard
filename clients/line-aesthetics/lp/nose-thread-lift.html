Your visit

What happens at your appointment

  1. Assessment

    Dr Ram checks three things first: skin thickness over the bridge, the strength of your tip cartilage, and the blood vessels in the area.

  2. Threads placed

    Under local anaesthetic, fine dissolvable threads are guided along the bridge and into the tip. It takes about 20 to 30 minutes.

  3. Settling

    You see the lift straight away. Over the following weeks collagen forms around the threads as they dissolve.

Suitability

Is it right for your nose?

Threads work with your own anatomy. Not every nose is suited, and Dr Ram will tell you honestly if yours isn’t.

It can

  • Raise a low or flat bridge
  • Lift and define a rounded tip
  • Straighten the profile line
  • Leave no visible scars

It won’t

  • Remove a large hump or narrow bone
  • Last forever; a top-up keeps the result
  • Suit every nose, which is why assessment comes first

Questions

What people ask Dr Ram

Does it hurt?

The nose is numbed with local anaesthetic first. Most people describe pressure rather than pain.

How much downtime is there?

Most people return to normal activities within a day. Some swelling or bruising is possible in the first few days.

How long does it last?

Typically 12 to 18 months. A top-up before the result fully fades usually needs fewer threads than starting again.

I’ve had filler or surgery on my nose. Can I still do this?

Possibly. Previous procedures change the blood vessel and tissue picture, so Dr Ram reviews your history carefully before recommending anything.

Threads or filler for the nose?

Threads lift and define, especially at the tip. Filler adds height and volume. Some noses do best with one, some with both.
